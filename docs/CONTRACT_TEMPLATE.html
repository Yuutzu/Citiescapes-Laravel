Penalty Clause

A grace period of 3 days applies after the bill's due date. Beginning on the 4th day overdue, a penalty of ₱100.00 per day is applied to the outstanding balance and continues to accrue until the bill is fully paid. Bills overdue beyond 14 days are escalated to Delinquent status; beyond 30 days to Eviction, after which the General Manager may terminate this contract at their sole discretion.

House Rules & Conditions

  1. Occupancy. The room shall not exceed the stated maximum number of occupants without prior written consent from the Lessor.
  2. Quiet Hours. Quiet hours are observed between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM. Noise complaints may be lodged through the tenant portal.
  3. Subletting. The Lessee may not sublet, assign, or share the leased room with persons not listed on this contract.
  4. Maintenance. The Lessee shall report damage or maintenance issues promptly via the My Requests page in the tenant portal.
  5. Utilities. Electricity, water, and Wi-Fi consumption is billed monthly based on actual readings and is due alongside the monthly rent.
  6. Common Areas. Hallways, stairwells, and shared facilities shall be kept clean and unobstructed at all times.
  7. Smoking & Pets. Smoking inside the room is prohibited. Pets are not permitted without prior written approval.
  8. Visitors. Overnight guests beyond two consecutive nights require prior notification to the Lessor.
  9. Termination. Either party may terminate this contract with thirty (30) days' written notice. Early termination by the Lessee forfeits the security deposit unless mutually agreed otherwise.
  10. Move-Out. Upon termination, the Lessee shall return the room in its original condition (normal wear and tear excepted) and return all keys to the Lessor.
  11. Security Deposit. The security deposit shall be refunded within fifteen (15) business days of move-out, less any outstanding balance, damage assessment, or unpaid utilities.
  12. Governing Law. This contract is governed by the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, and any disputes shall be settled in the courts of Davao City.
